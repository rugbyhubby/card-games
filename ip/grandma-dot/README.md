# Grandma Dot

**Original character by the repository owner.** First described 11 November 2026.

## Who she is

- A fictional Midwestern church-basement Euchre shark, and **your partner**. She sits
  across from you, so your mistakes cost her tricks, and she takes them personally.
- **Funny, patient, salty.** She roasts your *play* hard ("Stupid. Beautiful, but stupid.")
  but **never loses her temper**. The worse your play, the calmer she gets.
- A true catastrophe ends with her taking you to the kitchen to make **sugar cookies**.
- **Bad memory:** "Leave now and I'll forget all of this. I'm old, not patient."
- **"Grot"** is her family nickname, and it's earned. Her name tag says *Grandma Dot*
  until you graduate. Then your certificate is signed *Grot* and her tag changes.
- **Lou** is her late husband, who will one day coach Cribbage
  ("A hint? Lou never needed a hint. He needed glasses."). See [`../lou/`](../lou/).

## How she looks

- Late 70s, tight curly perm.
- **Cat-eye glasses on a beaded chain.**
- A button-up cardigan over a blouse with a **rounded Peter Pan collar**, plus small round earrings.
- A **coffee mug** always nearby.
- For catastrophes, a **flowered apron** and a **rolling pin**.
- Art style: bold, hand-inked black lines with no color, like a humorous greeting card.

## How she works in the game (Learn Euchre with Grandma Dot)

- Six lessons: the bowers, follow suit, partner's tricks, calling trump, going alone,
  defense. After them comes a graduation game: win a full game with her to graduate.
- **The napkin:** one mark for each putz play. Each time, the napkin comes out big with
  the reason written on it. Three marks in one lesson, or getting euchred, means a short
  trip to the kitchen (about 3 seconds, tap to skip), which says why you're there.
- **Hint:** unlimited, with a new remark each time. **Take it back:** once per hand, but
  the napkin mark stays. **"Why?":** tap her after a putz play.
