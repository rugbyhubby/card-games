# How the drawn Dot was made

- **Tool:** OpenAI's image tool (ChatGPT), used by the owner.
- **Prompt:** written by Claude Code for the owner on 12 November 2026, below. The owner may
  have adjusted it in the chat; the original chat is the final record.
- **Result:** one sheet with nine poses, in [`art/`](art/). The final art has no color
  (bold black lines), which is what the games use.

```text
Create a character sheet for an original cartoon character: "Grandma Dot," a sharp-witted Midwestern grandmother in her late 70s who plays cards in a church basement. She is funny, patient and a little salty, never angry.

Style: humorous greeting-card cartoon. Loose, confident hand-inked black outlines with slight wobble, like a felt-tip pen. Simple shapes, big expressive head (about one-third of her height), minimal shading. Limited flat colors only: soft lavender cardigan, white blouse, silver-gray hair, warm skin tone, one accent color on her mug. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime. Friendly and a little goofy, but more finished than a doodle.

Her look (keep identical in every image): tight curly silver perm; cat-eye glasses on a beaded chain that hangs around her neck; button-up cardigan over a blouse with a rounded Peter Pan collar; small pearl earrings; a plain white coffee mug with one stripe that she always has nearby.

Draw her nine times, same character, same outfit, same scale, upper body (waist up), facing slightly toward the viewer:
1. Neutral, sipping coffee, eyes half-closed and content.
2. Leaning forward, pointing down at something on the table, glasses slid down her nose.
3. A small approving nod with a closed-eye smile.
4. One eyebrow raised, unimpressed, mug held low.
5. Face-palm: one hand over her eyes, mouth flat.
6. Wearing a flowered apron, holding a wooden rolling pin across her chest, calm serene smile (a "we're going to the kitchen" look).
7. A slow, sarcastic clap, hands together in front of her chest, slight smile.
8. Smug: chin up, eyes half-lidded, smirking, holding up one playing card (a Jack).
9. Impatient: drumming her fingers on a table, looking sideways at the viewer.

Technical: each pose as its own square image, 1024x1024, transparent background (PNG), same framing so her head is in the same spot in every image. Give her a thin white outline around the whole figure (like a sticker) so she reads on both white and black backgrounds. Her face must still read clearly when shrunk to a 50-pixel circle. No text, no logos, no borders, no extra characters.
```

## For more poses later

Attach the original sheet and say: "Same character, same style, same line weight, no color.
New pose: …". Keeping every pose on the same style keeps her consistent, which also matters
for protecting her as a character.
